Omni Finance puts household spending and longer-term finances in one place. Lunch Money remains the source for transactions; this app adds the views and tagging workflow I wanted around it.

Transactions can be assigned to an owner or split. Budget views compare spending against targets, while trends make it easier to look across months and years. Amazon order imports add context to transactions that would otherwise just say Amazon.

The app also tracks account snapshots and includes a retirement projection. Those projections are scenarios based on assumptions, not a promise that I can stop working on a particular Tuesday.

It is a React frontend with a Python serverless backend. The source is private.

[Open Omni Finance](https://finance.jabdy.com). Sign-in is required.
