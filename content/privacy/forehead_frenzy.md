## Privacy Policy for Forehead Frenzy Android App

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Forehead Frenzy does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- The app works entirely offline and has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
- The motion sensor (accelerometer) is used only on your device, during a round, to detect when you tilt the phone. Sensor data is never saved or sent anywhere.
- Your settings, favorites, and the list of cards you've already seen are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- The app does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
